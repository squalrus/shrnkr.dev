HOW IT WORKS

Four proprietary compression passes

Semantic Concatenation Engine™

Fuses adjacent words into a single compact token — same meaning, fewer characters on the invoice.

Adaptive Vowel Elision

Drops the vowels your model never needed to infer intent, scaled to how aggressively you want to save.

Sentence Flattening

Strips punctuation tokens that add cost without adding information.

Redundant Phrase Detection

Flags filler like "just wanted to check in" and "to be honest" and removes it before it ever reaches your model.
