How it works

Six proprietary compression passes

Each pass removes cost. None of them removes meaning. That's the whole trick.

01

Semantic Concatenation Engine™

Fuses adjacent words into a single compact token — same meaning, fewer characters on the invoice.

02

Adaptive Vowel Elision

Drops the vowels your model never needed to infer intent, scaled to how aggressively you want to save.

03

Sentence Flattening

Strips punctuation tokens that add cost without adding information.

04

Redundant Phrase Detection

Flags filler like "just wanted to check in" and "to be honest" and removes it before it ever reaches your model.

05

Symbol Substitution

Swaps common words for compact symbols your model already understands — "move" becomes "→", "and" becomes "&". Same instruction, fewer characters on the invoice.

06

JDSL™

Just Drop Some Letters. Once your response is squished into a single word, JDSL deletes every 4th character outright — no exceptions. Engages exclusively at Meat Grinder (lvl 5) tier.
