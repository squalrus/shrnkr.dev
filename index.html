HOW IT WORKS

Four proprietary degradations

Semantic Concatenation Engine™

Fuses adjacent words into a single unbroken token-saving compound, regardless of whether a human can still parse it.

Adaptive Vowel Elision

Silently removes vowels once your account crosses a self-imposed "token pressure" threshold we invented.

Sentence Flattening

Strips punctuation on the grounds that periods, like everything else, cost money.

Confidence Padding

Reinserts the words "clearly" and "obviously" at no extra charge, to offset the parts that no longer make sense.
